About Me

I am a graduate student at Eastern Illinois University, completing an MBA and a Master of Sustainability in 2026. I work as a research assistant and teaching assistant, and I bring a background in marketing, procurement, and public-sector asset management from Nigeria and the United States.

My research looks at how artificial intelligence and technology affect emotional trust in the workplace, how digital narratives shape motivation and performance, and how sustainability messaging influences leadership and employee engagement. My master's thesis studies AI, technological support, and burnout among international students in the United States.

Outside of work I enjoy public speaking, hiking, swimming, movies, and creating content.
